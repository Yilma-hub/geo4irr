Geo4Irr Delaware

Geo4Irr gives you a starting point, not an instruction. Ground truth is your field. Check a probe or dig a hole before you act on anything here, especially in the first season.

Weather from the Delaware Environmental Observing System (University of Delaware) and the National Weather Service. Soils from USDA-NRCS SSURGO. Nitrogen guidance is a decision aid only — under the Delaware Nutrient Management Law, operations applying nutrients to 10 or more acres need a certified plan, and this tool does not replace it.
